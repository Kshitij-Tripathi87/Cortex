# Mutual NDA Template (Design Partner Pilot)

**Parties**:
- **Cortex** ("Disclosing Party"), a [jurisdiction] entity, [address]
- **Customer** ("Receiving Party"), a [jurisdiction] entity, [address]

**Effective Date**: _______________

## 1. Purpose

The parties wish to explore a 30-day pilot evaluation of Cortex's Supplier Disruption Decision Brief ("the Pilot"). Each party may disclose Confidential Information to the other solely for this Purpose.

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by either party, whether oral, written, electronic, or visual, that is:
- Marked "Confidential" or "Proprietary," or
- Reasonably understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure.

Includes, without limitation: technical data, trade secrets, know-how, financial information, customer lists, business plans, product roadmaps, and the specific disruption data provided for the Pilot.

Excludes information that:
(a) is or becomes public through no fault of Receiving Party;
(b) was known to Receiving Party prior to disclosure;
(c) is independently developed by Receiving Party without use of Confidential Information; or
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without breach of obligation.

## 3. Obligations

Receiving Party will:
- Use Confidential Information only for the Purpose.
- Disclose Confidential Information only to its employees and contractors with a need to know who are bound by confidentiality obligations no less protective than this Agreement.
- Protect Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information (no less than reasonable care).
- Not reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software provided.
- Not use Confidential Information to compete with Disclosing Party.

## 4. Data Handling (Pilot-Specific)

- Customer's supply-chain data (CSVs, exports) **never leaves Customer's deployment**. Cortex runs entirely on Customer-controlled infrastructure (on-prem or Customer cloud).
- Cortex does not retain copies of Customer data after the Pilot ends.
- Pilot outputs (Morning Brief JSON, backtest reports) are Customer's Confidential Information.

## 5. Term and Termination

- Term: 12 months from Effective Date.
- Either party may terminate with 10 days' written notice.
- On termination or Pilot completion, Receiving Party will return or destroy all Confidential Information and certify compliance in writing.

## 6. No License

Nothing in this Agreement grants any right, title, or interest in Confidential Information, except the limited right to use it for the Purpose. Cortex retains all IP in its engines, math, and software. Customer retains all IP in its data.

## 7. No Warranty

Confidential Information is provided "AS IS" without warranty of any kind.

## 8. Liability Cap

Neither party's aggregate liability under this Agreement exceeds **USD $10,000**, except for breaches of confidentiality (uncapped).
