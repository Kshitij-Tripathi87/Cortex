# Cortex Design Partner Kit

Welcome. This folder contains everything you need to evaluate Cortex as a
design partner.

## What's inside

| File | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| `ONE_PAGER.md` | The 90-second pitch. Hand this to a CFO. |
| `TECHNICAL_DEEP_DIVE.md` | The architecture and contract. Send this to your CTO/Head of Data. |
| `INTEGRATION_GUIDE.md` | The CSV connector spec. Send this to your integration engineer. |
| `ROI_CALCULATOR.md` | The cost-of-disruption worksheet. Use it with your COO. |
| `PILOT_AGREEMENT.md` | The 30-day pilot terms. Sign and return. |

## What we are asking for in exchange

- 4 hours of your team's time across the pilot.
- Two real disruption events (anonymized is fine) to backtest against.
- One reference call at the end of the pilot.

## What we are not asking for

- No procurement contract. No LOI. No exclusivity.
- No production data on our servers. Everything runs on your infra.
- No AI/LLM in the loop. Determinism is the product.

## How to start

1. Read `ONE_PAGER.md` first.
2. Skim `TECHNICAL_DEEP_DIVE.md` if you want the architecture.
3. Run `DEMO.md` from the repo root with your own UUIDs.
4. Sign `PILOT_AGREEMENT.md` and email it back.
