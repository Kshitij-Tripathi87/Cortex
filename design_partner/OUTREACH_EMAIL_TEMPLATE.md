## Template

Hi [Name],

I'm reaching out because [specific trigger: recent disruption / earnings call comment / supply-chain hiring / etc.].

**The problem in one line**: Mid-market manufacturers lose 2–4 days and $50K–$500K per supplier disruption because the data lives in 5 systems and the decision cycle is manual.

**What we built**: Cortex is a *decision brief*, not a dashboard. When a supplier goes down, Cortex answers:
- What's the impact? (revenue, margin, penalty, working-capital — 6 weighted scores)
- When does it hurt? (0/12/24/48/72h inventory depletion timeline)
- What do we do? (ranked actions by net benefit, traceable to the supply-chain graph)

Every number is reproducible. Same inputs → same outputs. No LLM in the loop.

**The ask**: We're running a 30-day design-partner pilot with 5 companies. You provide a historical disruption (or clearly labeled synthetic data) and 4 hours of team time. We run the backtest and show you the Morning Brief. You tell us if the numbers are something you'd act on.

No procurement. No platform commitment. Just one decision type: supplier delay / failure.

**Materials** (attached or linked):
- One-pager (90-second pitch)
- Technical deep dive (architecture + math)
- ROI calculator (your numbers, our model)
- Pilot agreement (30 days, free, NDA included)

Open to a 15-min call this week?

Best,
[Your name]
[Title]
