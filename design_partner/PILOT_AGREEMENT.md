## 1. Scope

Cortex will provide access to the MVP release for a period of **30 days**
from the effective date, including:

- The Morning Brief web application.
- The `POST /api/v1/briefs/supplier-failure` API.
- CSV connector and ingestion tooling.
- Two (2) backtest runs against Customer-supplied historical disruptions.

## 2. Customer obligations

Customer will:

- Provide a working CSV export of the supply-chain data described in
  `INTEGRATION_GUIDE.md`, or grant read access to a sandbox.
- Provide two (2) historical disruption events (anonymized if required) for
  backtesting.
- Participate in one (1) reference call at the end of the pilot.
- Spend no more than **four (4) hours** of team time across the pilot.

## 3. Cortex obligations

Cortex will:

- Provide the software on a Customer-controlled deployment (on-prem or
  Customer cloud).
- Run two (2) backtests and deliver a written report.
- Respond to integration questions within **one (1) business day**.
- Not transmit Customer data to any system outside Customer's deployment.

## 4. Licensing

The MVP is provided **as-is** for evaluation. No production license is
granted by this agreement. A separate license will be negotiated at the
end of the pilot if Customer wishes to continue.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party will treat the other's confidential information with the same
care it treats its own. This obligation survives termination for **two (2)
years**.

## 6. Term and termination

- Term: 30 days from the effective date.
- Either party may terminate with **five (5) days written notice**.
- On termination, Customer will stop using the software and delete all
  copies in its possession.

## 7. No warranty

The software is provided **without warranty of any kind**, express or
implied. Cortex's total liability under this agreement is limited to
**USD $1,000**.

## 8. No exclusivity

This agreement does not grant either party exclusivity. Customer is free
to evaluate competing tools. Cortex is free to work with other design
partners.
