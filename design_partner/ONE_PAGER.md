# Cortex — One Pager

## What it is

Cortex is a **decision brief**, not a dashboard. When a supplier goes down,
Cortex answers one question:

> *What is the impact, when does it hurt, and what do we do about it — in
> one screen, in one minute, with numbers your CFO can audit?*

## Who it is for

Mid-market manufacturers ($50M–$500M revenue) running 50–500 suppliers
where a single Tier-1 failure can stall a line for days.

## Why now

Disruption decision cycles take 2–4 days because the data lives in five
systems (ERP, MES, WMS, supplier portals, email). The cost is six figures
per incident.

## How it works

```
CSV upload ─▶ graph ─▶ propagation ─▶ impact ─▶ confidence ─▶ ranking ─▶ brief
```

Every number is reproducible. Same inputs, same outputs. There is no LLM
in the decision loop.

## What you get on day 1

- One screen: a Morning Brief with `Critical Alert`, `Business Impact`,
  `Timeline`, `Recommendations`, `Evidence`, `Decision Confidence`.
- Four role views (CFO, COO, Logistics, Procurement). Same numbers, different order.
- A reproducible backtest against your last disruption with held-out entities.

## What it does not do (yet)

- It does not connect to your ERP. CSV is the contract for the MVP.
- It does not generate recommendations. It **ranks** the actions your
  playbook already contains.
- It does not page a human. It tells the human what to do.
